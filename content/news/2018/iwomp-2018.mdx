2018年09月27日〜28日にSpain Barcelona で行われる The 14th International Workshop on OpenMP (IWOMP) 2018 にて，本研究室学生の渡部 (博士前期1年) が下記のタイトルで発表を行います．

> Yutaka Watanabe, Jinpil Lee, Taisuke Boku and Mitsuhisa Sato

At The 14th International Workshop on OpenMP (IWOMP) 2018, to be held in Barcelona, Spain from September 27 to 28, 2018, Watanabe (first-year master's student) of our laboratory will give a presentation with the following title.

> “Trade-off of offloading to FPGA in OpenMP Task-based programming”  
> Yutaka Watanabe, Jinpil Lee, Taisuke Boku and Mitsuhisa Sato

For the program and other details of IWOMP 2018, please see [here](https://iwomp2018.bsc.es/).
